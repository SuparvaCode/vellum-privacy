Short Summary: We Collect Nothing.

Vellum operates 100% locally on your device. We do not have backend servers, we do not run analytics or telemetry, and we never collect, log, store, transmit, or sell your browsing history, IP address, visited websites, or personal data.

01 Zero Data Collection Policy

Vellum ("the Extension") is engineered from the ground up as a local-first browser sanctuary. Every anti-fingerprinting disguise, ad-blocking rule, and malware heuristic executes strictly inside your own browser instance.

  • No Personal or Browsing Data Collected: Vellum does not collect, read, log, transmit, or share your browsing history, visited URLs, search queries, form inputs, cookies, authentication credentials, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • No External Servers or Analytics: Vellum makes zero outbound network connections to any developer server, cloud API, or third-party telemetry/analytics provider.
  • 100% On-Device Storage (chrome.storage.local): Your user preferences—such as whether the "Enhance Security and Block Ads" toggle is enabled, your active hardware disguise profile, and your personal list of whitelisted websites—are saved strictly inside your browser's local storage on your machine and never leave your device.
  • No Remote Code Execution: All JavaScript modules and Declarative Net Request (DNR) rulesets are bundled inside the signed Chrome Web Store package. Vellum never fetches or evaluates remote scripts or external filter lists.

02 How Browser Permissions Are Used Locally

To protect your browser across the web, Vellum requests only the minimum Manifest V3 permissions required to perform local network filtering and entropy masking:

Permission Strict Local Purpose
<all_urls> & scripting Required to inject Vellum's deterministic anti-fingerprinting hooks (Canvas, WebGL, WebAudio, Font Metrics, Navigator, Timezone) and surgical cosmetic ad-slot cleanup before third-party tracking scripts can fingerprint your device.
declarativeNetRequest & declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess Uses Chrome's native Manifest V3 Declarative Net Request engine to block known third-party advertising networks, cross-site tracking beacons, and strip tracking URL parameters (such as utm_*, fbclid, and gclid) locally.
storage Stores your toggle states, selected hardware disguise persona, session seed, and whitelisted sites locally on your computer via chrome.storage.local.
tabs & webNavigation Used locally to display the active tab's domain name in the Vellum popup for one-click site whitelisting, and to show a local security interception warning page if you navigate to a deceptive punycode or malware domain.
privacy Used locally to configure Chrome's built-in WebRTC IP handling policy (disable_non_proxied_udp) so websites cannot leak your local LAN or VPN-bypass IP address via STUN requests.
